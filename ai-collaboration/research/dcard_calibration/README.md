# Dcard Topic Calibration

This folder is for future topic calibration work, not large-scale scraping.

Rules for this folder:

- Do not collect personal data.
- Do not bypass login or anti-bot protections.
- Use only public content or manually supplied URLs or snippets.
- Store summarized notes, not full raw posts, unless explicitly needed and allowed.
- This task does not collect Dcard data.
- A later task may add semi-automated collection, but that is out of scope here.

Artifacts expected here:

- a manual or semi-automated topic calibration template
- a schema for structured calibration notes
- future summarized notes about patterns, hooks, and product-mapping opportunities
