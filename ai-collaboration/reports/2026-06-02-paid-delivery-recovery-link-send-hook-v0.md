## Hook Point

Selected hook point: after paid result completion is persisted and after the generation job is marked completed.

Reasoning:

- Entitlement and recovery contact binding happen earlier in paid delivery artifact creation.
- Paid result content is not ready until generation completes.
- Sending after completion avoids implying that browser return or payment verification alone is enough.
- The hook is non-fatal and cannot roll back the completed paid result.

## Eligible Contact Rules

Auto-send eligibility requires:

- `contact_type=email`
- encrypted Email value present
- transactional consent present
- exact `module_slug`, `analysis_result_id`, and `entitlement_id` match
- status is `bound` or `verified`
- source is one of:
  - `checkout_start`
  - `return_waiting`
  - `paid_ready`

Ignored:

- LINE contacts
- failed/revoked contacts
- contacts without transactional consent
- contacts without encrypted Email value
- completed-result/support/operator-test contacts for this automatic hook
- contacts that already have a recent unexpired sent Email recovery link for the same entitlement/contact/channel

Marketing opt-in is not required for transactional recovery Email.

## Send Behavior

For each eligible contact:

1. Create an Email-channel `paid_result_recovery_links` row.
2. Build a `/r/[recoveryToken]` URL in process memory.
3. Send through the configured Email adapter.
4. Mark the link `sent` only on provider success.
5. Mark the link `failed` on provider/config failure.
6. Treat existing unexpired sent links as duplicates and do not send again.

Raw `prl_` token, token hash, raw Email, `pa_`, `pcs_`, provider payload, and report content are not returned by the helper.

## Failure Behavior

- Email send failure is caught and summarized.
- Generation completion still returns success.
- `EMAIL_PROVIDER=noop` creates a recovery link but does not send and does not mark `sent`.
- Missing provider config does not fail paid delivery.
- Contact query failure returns an empty send summary and does not fail paid delivery.
