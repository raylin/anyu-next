# Experiment Spec v0 Execution Report

## Summary

Created Experiment Spec v0 for the `曖昧溫度計` fake-door test. The spec defines a lightweight demand-validation experiment for text-only relationship context input, free `曖昧溫度` results, and fake paid unlock intent for `下一句怎麼回`.

No product code, extraction code, prompt, schema, provider code, CLI behavior, sample outputs, database, analytics SaaS, payment, UI, or backend was changed or implemented.

## Experiment Direction Captured

- Core hypothesis: users will paste a short conversation or situation if promised a quick `曖昧溫度` result, and a meaningful subset will click fake paid unlock for `下一句怎麼回`.
- Primary situation types: `已讀不回`, `忽冷忽熱`, and `回訊變慢但看限動`.
- `前任` and `備胎` are kept out of primary acquisition hooks for v0.
- Paid fake-door CTA: `解鎖下一句怎麼回 — NT$49`.
- Recommended first landing-page copy: Variant B, pain-led.

## Technical Shape Captured

- Product runtime schema draft separated into `free_result`, `paid_preview`, `paid_result`, `share_card`, and `metadata`.
- Future product prompt candidate path documented as `prompts/product_result_prompt_v0.md`, but no prompt file was created.
- Minimal event model documented with local JSONL as sufficient for v0.
- Fake-door paywall state and manual fulfillment export concepts documented.
- Final technical stack remains intentionally undecided.

## Key Decisions Documented

- v0 remains text-input only.
- No screenshot upload, OCR, login, real payment, database, analytics SaaS, or subscription-first model.
- The fake-door click measures payment intent and should not charge users.
- Contact capture after fake-door click is optional but used for manual fulfillment.
- Success thresholds are directional and should be interpreted carefully with small sample sizes.

## Deviations From Handoff

- None.

## Remaining Uncertainties

- Which of the three primary situation types will convert best is unknown.
- Whether `NT$49` is the right fake-door price needs validation.
- Whether free results should be manual first or generated by a future internal prompt remains undecided.
- Required privacy reassurance before users paste context is unknown.

## Recommended Next Step

Review the Experiment Spec v0 in ChatGPT Web, then choose the first landing-page variant and decide whether free results should be manual or semi-manual for the fake-door test.
